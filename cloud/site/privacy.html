PILOT INFORMATION / NOVEMBER 12, 2026

Your data in the pilot.

PaperMarketing Cloud is an invitation-only pilot operated by Bonorasw. Contact the operator at arthur@bonora.com.br for access, support, export, correction, or deletion requests.

What the service stores

The cloud portal stores your name, email address, a password hash, invitation status, session records, and operational audit events. Your setup password is encrypted for the provisioning worker and removed from the queue after the application account is created. Essential session cookies keep you signed in. This site has no advertising trackers.

Your workspace

Each customer receives a separate application container, database, and file volume on shared server hardware. Administrators of the hosting service can access infrastructure and backups to operate the pilot. Containers are not dedicated virtual machines.

Providers and connections

When you connect an AI provider or another service, work you send to that service is subject to its own data handling and charges. No provider account is connected automatically. Review each connection before giving agents access.

Hosting and retention

The pilot uses a Hostinger VPS and Cloudflare DNS. Operational data remains while your account is active. Backup retention and any deletion request are handled by the operator; ask for the current backup policy and confirmation of deletion. No uptime or disaster recovery SLA is offered during the pilot.

Before general availability

Public pricing, commercial terms, support commitments, and expanded recovery policies will be published before paid self-service hosting opens. No payment is collected in this pilot.
